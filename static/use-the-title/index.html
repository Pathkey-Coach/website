Use it, or become it?

Two short readings on the same question: whether the role is something you are using, or something you have quietly become.

This is a mirror, not a measurement. Everything is worked out in your own browser — nothing is sent anywhere, stored, or compared against anyone else.

Eight statements. Answer for how things have actually been in the last few months, not for how you would like to be.
